Superstore Sales & Management Dashboard

📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail performance using the Superstore dataset. By examining sales data, product categories, and regional metrics, the goal is to evaluate overall business profitability, track key financial indicators, and provide actionable insights for strategic decision-making.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
Microsoft Excel:Data auditing, error-checking, data cleaning, and structured dataset preparation.
Power BI:Interactive dashboard creation, custom KPI summary cards, and comparative visual charts.

📈 Key Insights & Features
Executive Summary:Tracked total sales, total profit, and overall profit margins through dynamic KPI cards.
Category & Sub-Category Performance:Evaluated how different product lines contribute to revenue and where profit margins require optimization.
Regional & Market Breakdown:Analyzed sales distribution and geographic trends to highlight top-performing regions and monitor discount impacts.
